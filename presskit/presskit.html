Full Press & Storefront Description

You wake in a soot-stained 1890s crypt booth chained to an iron coin-pusher cabinet. Behind the brass peephole sits The Collector—an articulated bronze-skull automaton watching your ledger tick toward 00:00. Your left hand rests inside a 3-finger mahogany pillory beneath a honed steel guillotine blade.

Every drop is simulated at a deterministic 240Hz physics substep. Coins don't just slide—they teeter over the bevelled brass overhang lip, arc with Voltaic lightning chains, siphon Blood-Pact multipliers, and fuse directly on the playfield into 5 oversized Mythic Relics when three matching tokens collide.

Between escalating Quotas ($445, $920, $1,950+), open the Black-Market Draft Shop to bolt 14 physical Contraband devices onto the cabinet frame—from Brass Guardrails that rebound gutter losses to Piston Overclocks and Blood Magnets—and stamp tokens in the Alchemical Furnace with Gilded (2x Value), Lead-Core (+80% Mass), Volatile, or Twin-Echo brands. Miss a quota, and you choose which finger to sacrifice for a permanent blood-cursed buff—until all 3 fingers are gone.

History & Triple-Engine Architecture

WEBGL2 + GODOT 4.7 + UNITY 6

OBOL: The Cursed Coin Pusher began with a simple design question: Why do roguelike score-chasers almost always live on flat 2D card tables instead of heavy, tactile mechanical machines? To guarantee zero-friction accessibility for browser players and streamers while delivering uncompromising native desktop fidelity, we engineered OBOL simultaneously across three production runtimes sharing an identical 240Hz deterministic physics specification and 25-category procedural metal-plate synthesizer:

1. Three.js WebGL2 Browser Edition

Instant zero-install play inside any desktop browser at 60 FPS with a 240Hz fixed physics accumulator, PBR metalness/roughness shaders, and WebAudio physical-modeling synthesis. Perfect for live stream challenges and instant press evaluation.

2. Godot 4.7 Native (Vulkan Forward+)

Standalone 64-bit Windows executable powered by Godot 4.7's Vulkan Forward+ renderer, ACES Filmic tonemapping, SubViewport Collector Peephole, and native 44.1kHz procedural AudioStreamGenerator foley.

3. Unity 6 Native (PhysX 240Hz)

Standalone 64-bit Windows Direct3D11 build running Unity 6000.3 LTS at a locked fixedDeltaTime = 1/240s with 16 solver iterations, procedural metallic cookie textures, and sample-accurate OnAudioFilterRead acoustics.
